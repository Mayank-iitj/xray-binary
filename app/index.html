X-Ray Binary — Architecture & Judges Q&A

        
      

      

        
1. Why WL Kernel + GIN instead of bytes? Raw byte signatures and N-grams collapse under register renaming, instruction substitution, and relocation. Control-flow execution topology remains invariant.

        
2. What happens on packed malware? We detect packing honestly via Shannon entropy (>7.2) and W+X permissions. We report inconclusive rather than hallucinating false certainty.

        
3. How does Evasion Lab prove resilience? We apply 12 transformations (O0-O3, stripping, XOR loops, CFF) and demonstrate 91.7% structural retention where exact hashes drop to 0% and YARA drops to 16.7%.
